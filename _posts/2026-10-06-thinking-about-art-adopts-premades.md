## Thoughts on starting up the website and getting back into art
I've been wanting to make art for others for a while now. I feel fairly rusty but I am really excited to practice more! I've been doing digital art for years and have been practicing for a month or two now. Maybe the best step is to do some art adopts and see if the community would buy them/see if they are worth making premade fursuits of.
